002 / Who I Am
ABOUT
02

I'm Saarthak Singh, 18, and I've been editing video for 4 years — long enough to have a voice, young enough to still be reckless about it. I work at the intersection of narrative, rhythm, and visual aesthetics.

My editing isn't just about cutting clips. It's about controlling time, building tension, releasing it. Whether it's a short film, a brand reel, or a 90-second social cut — I make sure every frame earns its place.

I can shoot and edit, or either one independently. I understand light before I even touch a timeline. That dual fluency means you get a collaborator who speaks both languages — the language of the lens and the language of the edit.

Currently: Building. Learning. Obsessed.
